Bliss Wedding Catalogue

Bliss Giftings — Premium Marriage & Wedding Gifting Catalogue

This repository is for organising and developing the Bliss Wedding Catalogue. It is separate from the official Bliss Giftings business website, which will be developed later.

Purpose

- Organise wedding and marriage gifting products.
- Arrange products by category and occasion.
- Preserve existing product details, prices, and minimum order quantities.
- Make the catalogue easy to maintain and expand.
- Prepare the catalogue structure for future development.

Catalogue Guidelines

- Display confirmed product prices wherever available.
- Mark products without confirmed prices as Enquire for Price.
- Keep occasion-specific product groupings accurate.
- Preserve existing product information when adding or updating items.
- Keep Trousseau Packing as a decoration and packing service; customers must supply the products to be packed.

Project Status

Catalogue organisation and product-data reconciliation are in progress.

Note: This repository is for the wedding catalogue project, not the official business website.
